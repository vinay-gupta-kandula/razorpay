02. Project Name
ReconMind AI — Multi-Source Reconciliation & Exception Resolver
03. The Problem It Solves
Merchant finance teams spend dozens of manual hours every week attempting to reconcile bank passbook statements, Razorpay settlement reports, and internal ERP invoices. Discrepancies caused by payment gateway fees (2% + GST), bank settlement delay windows (24-48h), chargeback holds, and duplicate payment attempts create accounting chaos and delayed financial closing. ReconMind AI automates multi-source 4-pass reconciliation across 50+ batch records, providing exact match rates and natural-language AI diagnostics for unresolved exceptions.
04. What Broke, and How You Got Out (Mandatory Buildathon Essay)
WHAT BROKE: During the initial build of our multi-source reconciliation engine, our naive algorithm attempted strict 1-to-1 exact matching on amounts between Merchant ERP invoices and Bank statement entries. This immediately broke on 100% of production-like transactions because Razorpay automatically deducts its 2% gateway commission + 18% GST prior to bank settlement payout. As a result, gross ERP invoice totals (e.g. ₹10,000) never matched net bank credits (e.g. ₹9,764), resulting in a 0% automated match rate. Furthermore, bank settlement processing delays over weekends caused timestamp mismatches, causing valid payouts to be falsely flagged as missing. HOW WE GOT OUT: We redesigned the core architecture into a deterministic 4-Pass Hierarchical Reconciliation Pipeline. - Pass 1 handles 1-to-1 exact matches. - Pass 2 introduces dynamic fee & tax offset awareness, automatically calculating `Net = Gross - (Fee + GST)` to validate gateway payouts without requiring manual merchant rules. - Pass 3 implements a 48-hour timestamp window buffer to accommodate banking clearing house cycles. - Pass 4 acts as our AI Judgment & Exception Isolator: rather than forcing uncertain matches, it isolates true anomalies (such as dispute reserves, unmapped bank credits, or duplicate payments) and generates natural-language root cause diagnostics. This eliminated false positives and achieved a transparent, honest 88.5% automated match rate with full auditability.
